TabbyNotey privacy policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

TabbyNotey is a free personal project by Patrick Qi. It lets you attach notes to web page URLs. Your notes stay in your Chrome profile on your device.

What the extension handles

When you write a note, TabbyNotey saves its text, the full page URL, page title, a note ID, and creation and last-edited timestamps. Notes save as you type, including drafts before you click Add note. Full URLs include any query parameters and fragments, which may contain private information.

The extension reads open tabs’ URLs to show each tab’s note count on its toolbar badge and to find an already-open page when you return to it from All notes. It reads the active tab’s title to display and save it with a note. It does not keep a general browsing history log or use Chrome’s history API. It uses Chrome’s favicon service to display page icons.

TabbyNotey never reads or changes page content. It has no content scripts, analytics, advertising, account system, or cloud sync.

Storage, use, and sharing

Notes are stored only in chrome.storage.local for the Chrome profile and device where you use the extension. They are used to save, display, search, edit, and delete your notes, count notes on tabs, and return to saved pages. The extension makes no external network requests and does not transmit notes, URLs, or titles to Patrick Qi or any other service.

User data is not sold, shared for advertising, used for purposes unrelated to these features, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending. Patrick Qi cannot access notes stored by the extension on your device. TabbyNotey’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and retention

You can view, edit, search, or delete notes in the extension. Clearing a note’s text deletes that note. Notes remain locally until you delete them, clear the extension’s storage, or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes its stored notes.

Export lets you download a JSON copy containing your notes, full URLs, titles, IDs, and timestamps. The file stays wherever you choose to save it; the extension does not upload it. You control any later sharing or deletion of exported files. Exports are not removed by uninstalling. Import is not currently available.

Local browser storage is not an encrypted vault. Avoid storing passwords or other secrets in notes. Other people with access to your device or Chrome profile may be able to access them.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the extension, contact Patrick Qi at patrick.qi@gmail.com. If you choose to email, the information you send is handled through email so Patrick can respond; the extension does not send it automatically. Do not include private notes or sensitive URLs unless needed for your question.
